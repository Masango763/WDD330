📢 2026/2027 Seasonal Outlook & Early Warning Alert

The Department of Meteorological Services and Ministry advisories indicate that the upcoming 2026/27 November–March rainfall season will be influenced by El Niño conditions, pointing toward an erratic start and below-average rainfall across southern and western provinces. Farmers are strongly advised to prioritize moisture-retaining conservation practices and drought-tolerant seed varieties.

⚡ Active Recommendations: Scale up Pfumvudza/Intwasa basins, harvest winter wheat successfully, and secure water harvesting reserves.

💧 Rainwater Harvesting & Drip Irrigation

Techniques for smallholder retention basins, farm ponds, and gravity-fed drip systems to maximize water use efficiency during dry spells.

  • Construction of feya-feya and infiltration pits.
  • Low-pressure gravity drip kits for horticulture.
  • Mulching techniques to retain soil moisture.

🌱 Pfumvudza / Intwasa Conservation Farming

A climate-proofed agricultural production model designed to maximize yields on small plots through precision management.

  • Precise pothole digging (15cm x 15cm x 15cm).
  • Application of organic manure and basal fertilizers per station.
  • Timely weeding and crop residue retention.

🧪 Soil Health & Lime Management

Guidance on addressing acidic soils prevalent across Zimbabwe's natural farming regions to unlock nutrient availability.

  • Interpreting soil pH test results (target pH 5.5 - 6.5).
  • Agricultural lime application rates and timing before rains.
  • Crop rotation practices with legumes to fix nitrogen.

🐛 Pest & Disease Early Warning

Identification and management protocols for destructive pests affecting maize, small grains, and horticultural crops.

  • Fall Armyworm scouting and safe chemical/biological control.
  • Quelea bird monitoring protocols in small grain zones.
  • Stalk borer preventative seed treatments.
